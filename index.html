Chong Hua Hospital
Chong Hua Experience App

Healthcare UX · Patient Experience Design

Project Overview

The Chong Hua Experience project focused on reimagining the patient journey across Chong Hua Hospital's digital and physical touchpoints. The goal was to reduce friction in appointment booking, wayfinding, and post-consultation follow-ups — creating a seamless, human-centered healthcare experience for patients and their families. Chong Hua Hospital, the second-largest hospital in Cebu and a leading tertiary care provider in the Visayas region, needed a comprehensive digital platform to support both patients and healthcare professionals.

As UX Lead at Yondu, I spearheaded the design of a dual-access portal focused on streamlining appointment scheduling, medical records access, and clinical coordination. What began as a proof-of-concept (POC) pitch rapidly evolved into a full-scale digital health transformation, winning approval from the hospital's Board of Directors and securing a P15M contract for design and development.

Details

  • RoleLead UX Designer
  • IndustryHealthcare
  • PlatformWeb, Mobile
  • TimelineOctober 2024 – May 2025
  • TeamYondu x Chong Hua Hospital
  • ToolsFigma, Adobe Creative Suite

Design Process

🎯 Objectives


• Deliver a scalable platform with dedicated portals for patients, doctors, and administrators


• Reduce friction in appointment booking, prescription access, and patient records review


• Align UX with clinical logic, hospital operations, and data security protocols


• Lay the groundwork for future role-specific portals (e.g., nurses and medical assistants)


🧠 UX Leadership & Strategy


I led a team of designers from concept to execution, beginning with a winning pitch:


• Proof of Concept (POC): Co-led initial ideation, wireframes, and journey logic with two other designers for bidding presentation


• Board Approval: Our concept stood out for its clarity, scalability, and empathy for healthcare workflows, securing buy-in from Chong Hua’s leadership


• Design Kickoff: Official project design and development commenced in December 2024 following contract approval


📲 Core Experience Design


I led the full UX lifecycle across three primary user types:



🔹 Patient Portal


• Book appointments, view lab results, track billing


• UI optimized for mobile with icon-based navigation for older users


• Integrated SMS reminders and clinic schedules for accessibility



🔹 Doctor Portal


• Manage schedules, access patient records, prescribe medications


• Dashboard-first layout with quick access to urgent patient updates


• Enabled secure handoffs between departments and on-call teams



🔹 Admin Portal


• Track patient queue volumes, manage resource scheduling


• Oversaw design of internal dashboards for hospital operations


• Supported multi-level access control and bulk staff management


🔄 Future-Proofing: Nurse & Assistant Concepts

As a forward-thinking addition, I also developed concept designs for upcoming Nurse and Medical Assistant Portals. These served as groundwork for potential next-phase development, focused on:


• Real-time vitals and monitoring dashboards


• Triage handoffs and patient task tracking


• Communication tools between departments and support staff


Although not yet in development, these concepts were shared internally to position the platform as modular and extensible.


🚀 Outcome: Soft Launch & Impact


By February 2025, we successfully soft-launched the app on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store for limited live testing, gathering real-world feedback from Chong Hua patients and staff, implementing iterative design improvements, and establishing continuous feedback loops to support ongoing development. The project resulted in a ₱15M contract win, a production-ready multi-role healthcare platform, and a modular foundation for future medical portals. Today, Chong Hua Hospital’s digital platform serves as a benchmark for user-centered healthcare innovation in the Philippines, unifying patients, doctors, and administrators within a single digital ecosystem to enable faster care delivery, improved communication, and a more efficient hospital experience.

Project Overview

MAPS is a Math Academic Management System designed to support mathematics faculty, students, and academic coordinators in tracking curriculum progress, assessment performance, and instructional resources. The platform aimed to reduce administrative overhead while surfacing actionable academic insights.

The design challenge centered on creating a tool complex enough for faculty workflows yet intuitive enough for students to navigate independently.

Details

  • RoleUX Designer
  • IndustryEducation / EdTech
  • PlatformWeb Application
  • TimelinePlaceholder
  • TeamPlaceholder
  • ToolsFigma, Maze, Notion

Design Process

01 — Research

Conducted interviews with mathematics faculty, students, and academic coordinators across multiple institutions. Mapped existing workflows using process diagrams and identified redundancies in manual grade tracking, module assignment, and reporting.

🖼
Research & Workflow Maps

02 — Information Architecture

Built a multi-role IA accommodating three user types — student, faculty, and administrator — each with distinct navigation paths and data access levels. Card sorting sessions with 20 participants validated the taxonomy.

🖼

03 — Prototyping & Testing

Created mid-to-high fidelity prototypes for the grade dashboard, module builder, and assessment tracker. Conducted two rounds of usability testing and one moderated think-aloud session with 8 faculty participants.

Project Overview

The Globe Fiber Postpaid Microsite was a conversion-focused marketing experience designed to educate potential subscribers on Globe's fiber broadband plans, guide them through plan selection, and drive sign-up completions. The microsite needed to communicate speed, reliability, and value within a highly competitive telco landscape.

This project was part of the broader Globe digital product ecosystem at Yondu, contributing to a ₱15M contract win and significant engagement uplift.

Details

  • RoleUX Designer (Yondu / Globe)
  • IndustryTelecommunications
  • PlatformWeb Microsite
  • TimelinePlaceholder
  • Impact2,000% engagement uplift · +82.8 NPS
  • ToolsFigma, Adobe XD, Hotjar

Design Process

01 — Discovery & Competitive Audit

Analyzed competitor microsites across PLDT, Converge, and Sky Broadband. Conducted heuristic evaluations, reviewed existing Globe analytics, and identified drop-off points in the existing subscription funnel through session recordings and heatmaps.

🖼
Competitive Audit

02 — Content Strategy & UX Writing

Restructured the information hierarchy to lead with plan benefits over technical specs. Developed a clear, scannable content model that reduced cognitive load and guided users from plan comparison to checkout in fewer steps.

🖼
Content Model & IA

03 — UI Design & Prototyping

Designed a high-fidelity, responsive microsite aligned with Globe's brand system. Built interactive prototypes for stakeholder alignment and A/B testing preparation. Key focus areas: plan comparison module, speed visualizer, and subscription CTA flow.

🖼
UI Screens & Prototype

04 — Testing & Launch

Facilitated usability testing sessions with 10 target subscribers. Incorporated feedback into final design revisions, coordinated developer handoff, and supported QA for responsive behavior across mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints.

🖼
Testing & QA
